# Discipleship.Earth

**Rooted in the Word. Learning to follow Jesus Christ.**

[Visit Discipleship.Earth](https://discipleship.earth) · [Read our public introduction](https://github.com/Discipleship-Earth/discipleship-earth/blob/main/PUBLIC-README.md) · [Explore the Twelve](https://github.com/Discipleship-Earth/discipleship-earth/blob/main/profiles/README.md)

## Faith, learning, and a public record

Discipleship.Earth is being developed to help people explore biblical lives, examine Scripture, and reflect on what it means to follow Jesus Christ. The **King James Bible** is the foundation for our Scripture quotations and references.

We begin with the original Twelve disciples. Their profiles bring together biblical references, narrative, and visual storytelling, with an emphasis on making the sources and creative decisions available for inspection.

## How the work is developed

Our intended process is to identify supporting Scripture, develop the narrative and visual material, review the result, and document meaningful revisions.

Biblical statements, editorial inference, and artistic reconstruction should be clearly distinguished. Portraits and gallery scenes, including AI-generated images, are modern artistic interpretations rather than authenticated likenesses or photographs of biblical events.

As materials are prepared for public inspection, we intend to publish the relevant references, prompts, approved assets, and development notes. The editorial standards remain a proposal; their publication does not mean every live page has completed that review.

## A project in progress

The Twelve account profiles have been created. Complete biographies, galleries, and their supporting public records are being developed progressively. The live website may contain newer material than the documentation published here.

Follow the [content repository](https://github.com/Discipleship-Earth/discipleship-earth) to inspect the work and its revision history.
